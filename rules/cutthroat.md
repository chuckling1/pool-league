# Cutthroat

Usually three players, each with a group of five balls. **Protect your own balls and sink everyone else's.** The last player with any balls left on the table wins.

## Groups

- **Low:** 1–5
- **Middle:** 6–10
- **High:** 11–15

## Rack

- 15 balls in a triangle, the 1 on the foot spot.
- The 6 and 11 in the back corners.
- Everything else goes anywhere.

## Picking groups

The group can be decided either way. Agree before the break.

- **Before the break:** players pick groups in shooting order.
- **After the break (more common):** the table is open. The first player to legally pocket a ball after the break takes that group. The next player picks from what's left.

## Shooting order

Set the order before you start (lag, or rock-paper-scissors) and keep it for the whole game.

## Your turn

- Any ball can be hit first, including your own.
- You keep shooting as long as you pocket an **opponent's** ball.
- Sinking your **own** ball ends your turn, and that ball stays down.
- Once all five of a player's balls are down, that player is out.

## Fouls

- Scratching, missing everything, or no ball hitting a rail after contact.
- Penalty: each opponent who still has a ball down gets **one of their balls back on the table** (spotted on the foot spot). The next player gets ball in hand behind the head string.

## Winning

The last player with at least one ball on the table wins. When logging it, pick everyone who played and mark the winner. Each of the other players gets a loss.
