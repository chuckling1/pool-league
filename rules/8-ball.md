# 8-Ball

Two players. One shoots solids (1–7), the other stripes (9–15). Clear your group, then sink the 8 to win.

## Rack

- 15 balls in a triangle, the 8 in the middle of the third row.
- One solid and one stripe in the two back corners.
- The front ball sits on the foot spot.

## Break

- The cue ball goes anywhere behind the head string (the "kitchen").
- A legal break pockets a ball or drives at least four balls to a rail. Otherwise the opponent can re-rack and break, or play the table as it lies.
- **8 on the break:** the breaker wins, unless they also scratch (see House rules).
- Scratch on the break: the opponent gets ball in hand behind the head string.

## Choosing groups

- The table is **open** after the break, even if balls went in.
- Your group is set by the first ball you legally pocket after the break.

## Your turn

- You keep shooting as long as you legally pocket a ball from your group.
- Each shot must hit one of your own balls first. After contact, some ball must go in a pocket or hit a rail.

## Fouls (opponent gets ball in hand)

- Scratching (cue ball in a pocket or off the table).
- Hitting the opponent's group or the 8 first (unless you're on the 8).
- No ball hits a rail after contact and nothing goes in.
- Touching a ball with your hand, clothes or cue.
- Both feet off the floor.

## Losing the game

You lose if you:

- Pocket the 8 before your group is cleared.
- Pocket the 8 in a pocket you didn't call.
- Scratch while pocketing the 8.
- Knock the 8 off the table.
