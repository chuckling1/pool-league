# House rules

These are the rules everyone in the league agrees to. When the rules for a game type disagree with this page, **this page wins**. Edit this file in the repo whenever the group changes its mind.

## Logging games

- **The winner logs the game** right after it ends. If they forget, either player can do it.
- Log every game. A race (best of 3, and so on) counts as one game per rack.
- Logged the wrong thing? Hit **Undo** under Recent games within 24 hours and log it again.

## Table etiquette

- Lunchtime games come first during lunch. If people are waiting, play one game and rotate.
- Winner stays on only if nobody is waiting.
- Rack for each other: the loser of the last game racks.

## Choices we've agreed on

> Change these to match what your group actually plays.

- **Who breaks:** winner of the lag (both shoot to the far rail; closest to the near rail wins). After that, the loser of the previous game breaks.
- **Ball in hand:** after a foul the opponent can place the cue ball **anywhere on the table**.
- **Calling shots (8-ball):** call the 8 only. Other balls don't need to be called. Slop counts.
- **Scratch on the 8:** loss of game, unless it happens on the break.
- **Jump shots and massé:** allowed, but don't tear the felt.
- **Disputes:** ask whoever was watching. If nobody saw it, replay the shot.

## Sportsmanship

- Call your own fouls.
- No coaching during a game unless both players are fine with it.
- Have fun. It's lunch.
