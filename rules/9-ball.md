# 9-Ball

Two players, balls 1 through 9. You always have to hit the **lowest-numbered ball first**, but any ball can go in. Whoever legally pockets the 9 wins.

## Rack

- Diamond shape, the 1 at the front on the foot spot, the 9 in the center.
- The other balls go anywhere.

## Break

- The cue ball goes anywhere behind the head string.
- The break has to hit the 1 first and either pocket a ball or drive at least four balls to a rail.
- **9 on the break wins** (as long as it's a legal break with no scratch).
- If a non-9 ball goes in, the breaker keeps shooting.

## Push out

- Right after the break, the player shooting next can declare a **push out**. They can then shoot the cue ball anywhere without having to hit the lowest ball.
- The other player then chooses who shoots next from that position.
- Skip this if your group thinks it's too fancy.

## Your turn

- Hit the lowest-numbered ball on the table first.
- After contact, a ball must go in a pocket or hit a rail.
- Pocket any ball on a legal shot and you keep shooting.
- Combinations count: hit the 1 into the 9 and it goes in, you win.

## Fouls (opponent gets ball in hand)

- Not hitting the lowest ball first.
- Scratching or knocking the cue ball off the table.
- No ball hits a rail after contact and nothing goes in.
- Touching a ball with your hand, clothes or cue.

If the 9 goes in on a foul, it's spotted back on the foot spot and play continues.

## Three fouls (optional)

Three fouls in a row by the same player loses the game. Warn them after the second.
